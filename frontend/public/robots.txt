# Poemunity robots.txt
#
# Policy, decided 2026-11-20: block AI *training* crawlers, keep AI *search*
# crawlers. Every vendor runs those as two separate user agents, and a training
# opt-out has no effect on whether you appear in that vendor's answers — while
# blocking the search agent removes you from them. See AGENTS.md, "AI crawler
# policy", for the reasoning and for which one costs which.
#
# Anything not named below falls into the `User-agent: *` group, so Googlebot,
# bingbot, DuckDuckBot, Applebot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot,
# Claude-User, PerplexityBot and meta-webindexer all stay allowed to crawl.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /profile
Disallow: /api/

# --- AI TRAINING CRAWLERS: excluded from model training ----------------------
# Each is its own group, and under RFC 9309 a crawler with its own group
# ignores the `*` group entirely — harmless here, because every group below
# disallows the whole site and so needs no other rule of its own.

User-agent: GPTBot
Disallow: /

# --- TRAINING CONTROL TOKENS -------------------------------------------------
# These are not crawlers. Google and Apple read the token and apply it to
# requests their real crawler already makes, so neither produces a request of
# its own: blocking them is a statement about training use, not quota relief.
# `Google-Extended` explicitly does not affect Google Search or AI Overviews,
# and `Applebot-Extended` explicitly does not affect Applebot/Siri.
